Zhaoqin Jiang (a.k.a. Kushim Jiang, 姜兆勤) is a Software Development Engineer at the [Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences](http://www.iscas.ac.cn/). He is an Invited Expert of the [Ideographic Research Group (IRG)](https://unicode.org/irg/) of [ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 2/WG 2](https://unicode.org/wg2/), and has long-term participation in [IRG](https://unicode.org/irg/), [UTC](https://unicode.org/consortium/techgloss.html), [UTC/CJK](https://unicode.org/consortium/committees.html) and [UTC/SEW](https://unicode.org/consortium/committees.html) meetings.

Since 2017, he has worked on the collation, review, encoding and standardization of rare Han ideographs used in personal names, place names and large dictionaries. See [About]({{site.baseurl}}/pages/about/) for his IRG, WG2 and UTC documents on G-source, UK-source and V-source characters. Since 2020, he has been engaged in the collation, encoding, font development and standardization of living minority scripts and ancient Chinese ethnic scripts, including [Mongolian]({{site.baseurl}}/pages/about/#mongolian), [Tai Xaau]({{site.baseurl}}/pages/about/#tai-xaau), [Classical Yi]({{site.baseurl}}/pages/about/#yi-ideographs), [Nüshu]({{site.baseurl}}/posts/2024-06-12-nushu/) and [Khitan Small Script]({{site.baseurl}}/posts/2024-07-12-kss/). His current research focuses on character sets for complex writing systems, font engineering, and text-shaping engine development.

This website does not maintain the information about the abstract shapes of the CJK unified ideographs in the _Abstract Shape_ section. This information is maintained in a separate repo called [Kushim-Jiang > abstract-shape](https://github.com/Kushim-Jiang/abstract-shape). Suggestions and comments regarding this information should be submitted in [Kushim-Jiang > abstract-shape > Issues](https://github.com/Kushim-Jiang/abstract-shape/issues).

This website also does not maintain the information of the Oracle Bone characters in the _Abstract Shape_ section. This information is maintained in a separate repo called [Kushim-Jiang > oracle-bone](https://github.com/Kushim-Jiang/oracle-bone). The glyphs and their numbers displayed in this section are all from [_Jiǎgǔ Wénzì Gǔlín_](https://search.worldcat.org/zh-cn/title/36355882). Suggestions and comments regarding this information should be submitted in [Kushim-Jiang > oracle-bone > Issues](https://github.com/Kushim-Jiang/oracle-bone/issues).
